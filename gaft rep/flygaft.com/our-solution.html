Sustainable aviation fuel from carbon dioxide

GAFT supplies a sustainable and valuable lipid feedstock to Sustainable Aviation Fuel producers



Our process explained

Our process has two main conversion steps:

 Our patented high-pressure CO2 electrolyser uses renewable electricity to directly convert non-fossil CO2 and water into a new energy carrier

 Subsequently, this energy carrier is utilised by microorganisms to produce lipids by fermentation, a natural process used for centuries to produce foods and beverages

 

 

Our value proposition

We deliver an abundant and better quality feedstock that has a much higher value than conventional lipids such as Used Cooking Oil, Tallow and POME.
